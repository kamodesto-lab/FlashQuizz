# Project Title: LvlUP

Description: 
   The Philippine Science High School System (PSHS System) implements a six-year STEM-based curriculum designed to develop students' scientific and technological capabilities, critical thinking, and other essential skills (Philippine Science High School System, academic setting, students may have different learning difficulties, study habits, and preferences for reviewing lessons. To examine these experiences among the students of PSHS-EVC, a survey was conducted to identify challenging subjects, factors affecting studying, preferred learning methods, and interest in game-based learning. In the survey, 16 out of 21 respondents (76.1%) reported that they sometimes or often struggle in undrestanding their lessons, indicating a learning need that our proposed platform could explore addressing. In response, this project proposes LvlUP, an experimental supplementary learning platform that incorporates interactive quizzes, game-based challenges, rewards, and progress tracking to make lesson review more engaging. The platform will be evaluated through user testing to assess its functionality, usability, and perceived usefulness as a

supplementary learning tool

Features: 
File Upload, AI-Generated Flashcards, AI-Generated Quizzes, Multiple Quiz Types, Regenerate Questions, Study Mode, Score Tracking, Save Study Sets

How to run the program: 
The user uploads their study materials, such as PDF files, pictures, or plain text, then chooses what they want to create, such as flashcards or quizzes. The integrated AI then analyzes the uploaded sources then generates the requested study material based only on the provided sources. The user can then study, answer, save, or regenerate the materials.
